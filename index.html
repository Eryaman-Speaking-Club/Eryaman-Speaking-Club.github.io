01 · The idea

English is not a subject here.
It is the room.

We created Eryaman Speaking Club for people who understand English but need a real place to use it. There are no grammar lectures, exams or forced performances.

Instead, we use guided questions, small-group conversations and playful activities to make speaking easier. You participate at your own pace, meet people from different backgrounds and leave with more confidence than you arrived with.

Speak freely. Listen closely. Grow together.

05 · Good to know

Questions before your first meetup?

We have probably wondered the same thing.

What level of English do I need?+

If you can understand everyday English and build simple sentences, you can join. Most activities work well from A2–B2+, and nobody expects perfection.

Can I come alone?+

Absolutely. Many people do. The activities and changing groups make it easy to meet others without forcing awkward introductions.

Is this an English course?+

No. It is a social speaking community. We create the setting and activities; you create the conversation.

What if I make mistakes or forget a word?+

That is normal—and exactly why the club exists. Explain it another way, ask for help and keep going. Communication matters more than flawless grammar.

Where do meetups happen?+

We meet at selected cafés around Eryaman. The current location, day and time are announced on our Instagram page before each meetup.
