03 · What happens at a meetup?

The rhythm of the evening.

Real conversation, rotating groups and low-pressure practice.

🗣️ Speak moreLess theory, more real conversation.🤝 Meet peopleNew tables and an easy-to-join community.🌱 Build confidenceMistakes are part of the practice.

Arrive, settle in, meet people

Grab your coffee, take a seat and meet people. Coming alone is completely normal.

+50 min
Warm-up conversations

Easy questions get everyone talking. Nobody is suddenly put on the spot.

06 · Before you come

Things you may wonder before your first meetup.

Here are the questions we hear most often.

What English level do I need?+

If you can understand everyday English and build simple sentences, you can join. Meetups are mainly designed around A2–B2+ levels.

Can I come alone?+

Yes. Many people come to their first meetup alone; table rotations and games make meeting people easier.

How much does it cost?+

Your first meetup is free. Current in-person and online prices are listed in the Participation section.

Where are the meetups held?+

In-person meetups take place at cafés and social venues in Eryaman. Online joining links are shared after registration.

How do online speaking meetups work?+

They are live group speaking-practice sessions. Choose “Online” in the form and the relevant date and joining link will be shared.

Is the first online meetup free too?+

Yes. Your first online speaking meetup is free.

Is this an English course?+

No. ESC is a social speaking community. We prepare the setting and activities; participants create the conversation.

Is online speaking the same as a private lesson?+

No. Online speaking meetups are group practice. One-to-one lessons are separate; see the private lessons page.
