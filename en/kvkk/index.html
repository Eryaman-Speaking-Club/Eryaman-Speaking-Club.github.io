Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) · November 18, 2026

Personal Data Notice

This notice explains how personal data may be processed when you register for a speaking meetup, contact us, or consent to optional website analytics.

Contact: Eryaman Speaking Club organizing team · eryamanspeakingclub@gmail.com

This is an information notice under Article 10 of the KVKK. It is not blanket marketing consent.

1. Data controller

The organizers operating Eryaman Speaking Club determine the purposes and means of personal data processing for club activities. You can reach the organizing team at the address above. The official identity or registered legal name of the person or entity acting as data controller must be confirmed against the organization's legal status.

2. Information we process

  • Registration: Name, email, selected meetup, registration and attendance status, date of submission.
  • Contact: Information and messages you choose to send us.
  • Site activity: Technical records necessary to operate the site; optional analytics information, session identifiers, device/browser data and approximate location inferred from IP where analytics is accepted.
  • Advertising: Optional advertising measurement and related cookies if you separately consent.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information in registration or contact messages.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use registration data to organize the meetup you request and send relevant changes or joining instructions, where required to enter into or perform the participation arrangement (KVKK Article 5/2(c)) or, where applicable, for legitimate interests under Article 5/2(f). Contact inquiries may be handled on these bases as applicable. Legal duties and rights requests may rely on Article 5/2(ç). Optional analytics and advertising cookies that require consent are used only based on a separate affirmative choice under Article 5/1. Event-related operational messages are not general advertising permission.

4. Collection methods

Data is collected electronically via the website's event forms, linked Google Forms, emails you send, and tools activated according to your cookie preferences. Registration information you provide during in-person events may also be recorded.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Where necessary for operating the service, data may be handled by hosting and technical service providers, Supabase, email and registration services such as Google Forms and Google Meet, and competent authorities when required by law. Meta tools may be enabled for users who choose advertising cookies. These services may use infrastructure outside Türkiye. Any international transfer must independently meet the requirements of KVKK Article 9; this notice itself is not consent to an international transfer.

6. Retention

Event and contact information is kept for as long as necessary for the relevant activity and applicable legal obligations, then deleted, destroyed or anonymized as required. For optional analytics and cookie preference retention, see our cookie information page (in Turkish).

7. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11, you may ask whether your data is processed, request details, learn its purposes and recipients, seek correction or deletion where applicable, and exercise other rights provided by law. Contact eryamanspeakingclub@gmail.com; other statutory application routes remain available.

8. Cookies and choices

Optional analytics and advertising choices are separate. You can view basic club content without accepting them and change your preferences on the cookie information page. Reading this notice does not constitute consent.
